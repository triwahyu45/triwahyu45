### 👨‍💻 About Me

🎓 **Undergraduate in Mechatronics Engineering Education** at **Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta (UNY)**. Passionate about bridging the physical and digital worlds through **Robotics, Embedded Systems, Computer Vision, and AI Automation**.

- 🤖 **Robotics Competitor & National LKS Mentor**: Actively designing autonomous mobile robots, drivetrain kinematics (Mecanum/Omni), and mentoring vocational school robotics teams.
- 👁️ **Computer Vision & AI Enthusiast**: Developing real-time object tracking with **YOLO**, camera telemetry, and interactive AI agent remote controllers.
- 🛠️ **Hardware & IoT Developer**: Designing embedded firmware with **ESP32, STM32, Arduino**, and CAD mechanics using **Autodesk Inventor & Fusion 360**.
- 🚀 **Interactive Web Creator**: Building lag-free web simulators with **HTML5 Canvas, WebGL, Three.js**, and vanilla high-performance architectures.
